Incentives

Check eligibility before planning

Targeted incentives can support qualifying projects. Eligibility depends on the activity, location and investment structure.

Corporate tax relief

The playbook notes 5% corporate tax for 10 or 15 years for qualifying manufacturing and service activities, subject to specified investment thresholds. Playbook pp. 3, 14-15

Talent tax advantage

The playbook notes 5% personal income tax for knowledge workers as a possible benefit for skilled professionals recruited under the JS-SEZ. Playbook pp. 3, 26

Watch the tax and operations details

  • Cross-border work can create personal tax and work-pass issues.
  • A Johor presence can affect permanent-establishment and transfer-pricing analysis.
  • Customs data, rules of origin and supporting documents must stay consistent across both sides.
Playbook pp. 14-18

Talent and travel

Plan staff mobility early

Pass and tax obligations differ by role, activity and time spent in Johor. Confirm current requirements with the relevant authority. Playbook pp. 23, 27-31

EP

Employment Pass

MIDA assesses expatriate posts for approved manufacturing and selected service-sector companies, and supports applications through the Xpats Gateway system.

PVP

Professional Visit Pass

A temporary pass for foreign professionals providing a defined overseas-company service in Malaysia, such as training, consultancy or project work.
